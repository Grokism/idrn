Background

The International Disaster Response Network is a global relational network that was established in 2007. IDRN Global, is a nonprofit 501c3 organization based in the United States of America that was established in Texas in the fall of 2024 specifically focused on facilitating the growth of the existing relational network.

IDRN Global is not the entire network. It is a dedicated organization focused on facilitating and supporting the entire network by encouraging awareness, highlighting best practices, and promoting new and better levels of collaboration among response groups and networks around the world.

IDRN Global is not intended to be the same as IDRN USA or IDRN United States. IDRN Global operates as a peer to the historical functional IDRN groups that have persisted in Southeast Asia and East Asia through the years.
